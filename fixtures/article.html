A quieter city

By Mira Chen

October 20, 2026

A community garden opened beside the station this autumn. Volunteers transformed an unused strip of pavement into a shared space for herbs, flowers, and conversation.

The gardeners meet every Saturday morning to plant seedlings and water the raised beds. Neighbours donate tools and exchange practical advice about the changing weather.

Local shops have offered compost and seeds to keep the project growing. The group records its harvest each month and shares the vegetables with nearby households.

The next planting session will focus on native flowers that support pollinators. Everyone is welcome to visit the garden during daylight hours and learn about the plants.
